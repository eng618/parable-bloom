**Full Description (Max 4000 chars)**

> Find peace in puzzles. Parable Bloom is a Christ-centered arrow puzzle game where you guide directional vines off the grid in Snake-like movement — every segment follows as a queue, and clearing one vine can unblock the next.
>
> HOW TO PLAY
> • Tap a directional vine to slide it in its head direction
> • Body segments follow as a queue — plan your path
> • Clear every vine to bloom the level
> • Blocked vines animate back with scriptural encouragement — God's grace is endless, try again!
>
> FEATURES
> • 105 hand-crafted levels across 5 modules, unlocking the parables of Jesus
> • Grace system: 3 Grace per level (4 on Transcendent) — no harsh game-overs
> • Four difficulty tiers: Seedling, Nurturing, Flourishing, Transcendent
> • Adaptive light and dark watercolor garden themes
> • Scripture journal that unlocks as you progress
> • Offline-first: play anywhere, progress syncs when you are back online
>
> A moment of calm for your busy day — where logic meets devotional reflection.
